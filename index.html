Architecture practice

What I design — not just operate. These are the decisions that shape a platform before a single pipeline runs.

SECURE SDLC

Shift-left by design

Threat-model the pipeline first: SAST/DAST gates, dependency and secret scanning, and remediation SLAs built into the flow rather than bolted on after audit.

ZERO TRUST

Access architecture

PAM, MFA, ZTNA, RBAC and least-privilege IAM designed as one identity model across environments — so access is provable, not assumed.

PLATFORM

Cloud landing zones

Azure and AWS foundations: network segmentation, secrets management, and container orchestration (AKS/EKS) laid out for isolation, scale, and clean blast radius.

POLICY-AS-CODE

Repeatable infrastructure

Terraform, Ansible and Helm modules with guardrails encoded in code — drift-controlled, reviewable, and audit-ready by default.

COMPLIANCE

Audit-ready posture

PCI, VA/VIT, CNAPP and SonarQube cycles designed into the delivery cadence, with RCAs and evidence prepared for client security teams ahead of ask.

RESILIENCE

Reliability & DR

Observability (Prometheus, Grafana, Datadog), autoscaling, DR readiness and L2/L3 incident design that keeps six critical apps inside SLA.
